# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Solum project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

Participants should be respectful, assume good faith, welcome different viewpoints, give and receive constructive feedback, and focus discussion on the work and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct that would reasonably make participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
